## 2. Bull Case / Bear Case

**Bull case:**
- Diversification away from single-client concentration (non-lighting 73.5% of revenue, +52.8% YoY, up from 57.2% in FY25)
- Greenfield capacity inflection — ₹297.3 Cr of ₹326.1 Cr net IPO proceeds deployed; ~5 lakh sq ft Noida footprint; entering asset-sweating phase
- High-barrier verticals scaling: automotive (5 Tier-1 OEM approvals, 18-24 month qualification cycles), Honeywell industrial/BESS/BMS adjacency, wearables ODM value shift
- Balance sheet strength: D/E 0.07, interest coverage 9.1x; long-term EBITDA margin target 17-18% (from 13.0%) as utilization reaches 50-60%

**Bear case:**
- Severe cash flow conversion weakness: FY26 CFO ₹16.4 Cr vs PAT ₹41.6 Cr (0.39x); ₹334.1 Cr locked in working capital; cash conversion cycle >225 days
- Sequential margin compression (274bps, 15.7%→13.0%) from employee cost jump (+15.5% QoQ) and spot component buying; semiconductor lead times >12 months; copper/aluminum inflation
- Conservative FY27 guidance (18-20%) vs. 41.1% YoY Q1 print implies possible H2 deceleration
- Subsidiary governance/disclosure gaps: unlisted subsidiaries (Royalux Exports, Royalux Lighting, IKIO Solutions) ~73.5% of consolidated revenue; joint statutory auditor resignation (July 2026); single-segment reporting under Ind AS 108 limits vertical-level margin visibility; standalone permanent employee turnover 49% (worker turnover 43%) in FY26
- Valuation (31.9x TTM P/E, 33.6x annualized) outpacing current return ratios (ROCE 11.8%, ROE 7.8%, as stated in this response — note: differs from the 10.0% FY26 ROCE given in the overview table, see Discrepancies section below)

**Core pivot variables identified:** Bull — speed of automotive OEM ramp-up and Honeywell SKU commercialization. Bear — CFO/PAT conversion improvement and working capital normalization.
